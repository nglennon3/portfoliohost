Visual design

Abstraction

Abstract background visuals for a live orchestra performance made with TouchDesigner.

Description

Abstraction was a unique project to explore. We were challenged to create a five part animation in our groups, I took two parts to do. Touch Designer was the software we used for our animations, as they needed to be abstract and without distinct shapes.

Motivation

I felt connected to this musical piece
